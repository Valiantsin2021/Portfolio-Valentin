# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`, `assets/css/style.css`, `assets/js/main.js`). No framework, no build step. Hosted as a static site (GitHub Pages). Confirmed constraint: keep it this way.

## Users

- **Hiring managers and executives** (Director/VP level) evaluating Valentin for QA leadership or AI-in-QA roles. They need to see scope, measured impact and leadership approach fast.
- **Tech recruiters** screening quickly: title, location, impact numbers, stack keywords.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Valentin Lutchanka, Agentic QA Engineering Manager (Málaga, Spain). It shows how he brings AI agents into the software testing lifecycle and what that measurably saved. Success means the visitor reaches out via email or LinkedIn.

## Positioning

A QA leader who came up through every rung (manual tester 2018 → automation → SDET → manager of 24) and now runs AI adoption in testing with Lean Six Sigma discipline: baseline first, then agents, then control. Every claim is a measured number against a manual baseline, not an AI buzzword.

## Operating Context

- Read on desktop during a hiring process and on phone from a LinkedIn or recruiter link.
- Sections: Impact, AI in QA (five STLC stages), Leadership, Experience, Toolkit, Credentials (certification images), published tools, Contact.
- Contact paths: email `lutchenkovalentin@gmail.com`, LinkedIn, GitHub (`Valiantsin2021`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static single page; light and dark themes with persisted toggle (`localStorage`), both must stay supported.
- Impact numbers are fixed facts and must never be reworded, rounded or inflated: **3,500+** labor hours saved, **24** SDET/UAT analysts led, **+70%** testing efficiency (700+ hours yearly), **2×** faster deployments, 20% execution and maintenance cuts at EPAM.
- Employer (Verisk Underwriting Solutions) is named neutrally. No Verisk logos, colors or branding on this personal site.
- Schema.org `Person` JSON-LD and OG meta present; keep in sync with on-page facts.

## Brand Commitments

- Voice: first person, plain and concrete, measured claims, no hype. People stay in the loop; agents take repetitive work.
- Name: Valentin Lutchanka (LinkedIn handle uses "Valiantsin").

## Evidence on Hand

- Portrait: `assets/img/profile-img.jpg`; hero background `assets/img/hero-bg.jpg`.
- Certifications: `assets/img/certifications/` (ISTQB, LangGraph, Lean Six Sigma context, management courses, numbered certs).
- Tool screenshots: `assets/img/portfolio/` (ROI calculator, API assertions generator, playwright-performance-metrics, cypress-performance).
- Published tools: Test Automation ROI Calculator, API Assertions Generator, playwright-performance-metrics, cypress-performance, newman-parallel, Zod Schema Generator.
- No testimonials, client quotes, press or talks on hand. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Every claim carries its number; numbers come only from the confirmed list.
2. Optimize for the 30-second scan by a recruiter and the 3-minute read by a hiring manager.
3. Contact is always one step away.
4. Show the method (measure, introduce agent, control), not just the AI label.
5. Personal site first: employer is context, not brand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep existing basics: skip link, labelled icon buttons, alt text, theme respecting `prefers-color-scheme`. No product-specific standard set beyond that.
